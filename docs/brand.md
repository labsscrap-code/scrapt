# Brand and UX direction

## Identity

**Name:** SCRAPT

**Headline:** CLARITY IN MARKET CHAOS.

**Product description:** Real-time market intelligence. Quantitative signals. Data-driven decisions.

## Visual direction

The approved direction combines Swiss editorial minimalism, strong typography, architectural grids, abstract modular geometry and carefully structured whitespace.

The landing experience uses a light monochromatic palette. The analytical workspace uses near-black and neutral charcoal surfaces. Green and red may communicate market semantics; decorative neon effects and excessive motion are outside the intended direction.

## Interaction principles

- Use clear hierarchy and readable data presentation.
- Preserve useful information while improving layout and navigation.
- Provide keyboard access and visible focus states.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Distinguish loading, failure and unavailable-data states.
- Keep claims factual and grounded in verified functionality.

## Scrappy and public assets

Scrappy remains the product's existing assistant and character. This repository does not distribute the character artwork, application screenshots or a downloadable brand asset pack. Additional assets require explicit public-release approval and a rights review before publication.
